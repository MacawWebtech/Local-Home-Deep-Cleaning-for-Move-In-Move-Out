BEFORE YOU ASK

A few useful answers.

What affects the final price?

Property size, bathroom count, condition, access and selected add-ons all affect scope and price. The online amount is an indicative estimate.

Is a requested date confirmed?

No. Your preferred date and time must be reviewed before an appointment can be confirmed.

Are all tasks included in every package?

No. Interior appliances, cabinet interiors, additional bathrooms and extra areas require an agreed add-on scope.
