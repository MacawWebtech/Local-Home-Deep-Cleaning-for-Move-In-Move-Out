    07 / GOOD QUESTIONS

    A few things
    you might ask.

    Planning around a moving date? Share your property size, access details and any rooms that need extra care. A clear scope makes the next step easier.

    Before the cleanConfirm access, parking and the areas to cover.
    During the cleanFollow an agreed checklist for each room.
    After the cleanReview the completed scope before handover.
    What’s included in a deep clean?

    Accessible kitchen and bathroom surfaces, dusting, room surfaces and floors. The exact checklist depends on your package and property. Appliance interiors, cabinet interiors and additional spaces can be discussed as add-ons.

    Does my home need to be empty?

    Empty homes make move-in and move-out cleaning easier. Furnished properties can also be discussed; please share access limitations and any belongings that need special care.

    Is the estimated price the final price?

    No. Estimates are indicative. Size, bathroom count, property condition, selected add-ons and access affect the final quote, which must be agreed before confirmation.

    Can I choose my cleaning date?

    You can submit a preferred date and arrival window. The request remains awaiting confirmation until availability and scope are reviewed.

    Are appliance interiors included?

    Oven and refrigerator interiors, cabinet interiors and similar detailed tasks are optional add-ons. Include them in your enquiry so they can be added to the quote.

    Will cleaning guarantee my deposit return?

    Deposit decisions belong to your landlord or property manager. Cleaning can help prepare your home for handover, but it cannot guarantee a refund or the removal of every stain.
